# Experimental release - 1 October 2026

This update repairs the completed ACC Rival guide that could strand Development
and iDroid input, incorporates Coco's scope-glass stabilization, and separates
the extra optics camera's brightness history from the ordinary head view.
It also adds live 3D fitting for VR settings and optional prompts that reflect
the player's saved controller bindings.

Supported game: The Phantom Pain 1.0.15.4, Windows x64, PC OpenXR headset.
This is an experimental prerelease. Full-game completion, every upgrade/menu
permutation, every weapon and physical headset acceptance remain open.

## Changes

- The completed ACC **Choose a Rival** guide now releases its own stale pause
  and restores the menu restrictions it borrowed. Existing independent locks
  and unfinished tutorials remain owned by the game. Ordinary root Back returns
  to the live cabin, and physical iDroid can reopen immediately.
- **Steady scope glass** defaults on. Coco's correction stabilizes native ocular
  drift relative to the aiming hand and moves the objective with it. Optional
  weapon smoothing remains a separate setting.
- The magnified lens has independent native CPU luminance textures, GPU
  readbacks and adaptation history. Aiming the optic no longer feeds that lens
  history into the head camera; ordinary head adaptation remains active.
  Isolation defaults on for supported TPP and does not modify Ground Zeroes.
- The launcher offers live 3D reference fitting for all 55 VR adjustments,
  including iDroid hand/device fit, screen origin, distance, width and rotation.
  The iDroid screen uses a lower-edge anchor above its emitter. The retail light
  cone still needs alignment work for custom screen offsets and rotations.
- **One-hand weapon tilt** defaults to -30 degrees. Gun, firing hand, physical
  sight and muzzle share the corrected wrist. Existing nonzero general hand
  pitch does not receive another automatic trim; explicit weapon tilt wins.
- Optional **VR control prompts** use effective personal bindings. Map's
  Place/Remove Marker caption suppresses the stock Confirm graphic, supports
  remapped chords and restores the native graphic in native-button mode.
  Other prompt families remain a verification queue.
- Ordinary status and HUD stay on the **left bionic forearm**. The right hand
  holds iDroid. Physical optics and binocular aiming, zoom, acquisition and
  marking are preserved; floating aiming overlays remain off.
- Desktop preview stays **1280 x 720**. VR uses the active runtime's recommended
  per-eye size and eye FOVs independently of the desktop preview.
- Local development refreshes the same **play/** folder. The maintained
  simulator launcher uses the existing Steam client and temporarily selects
  the runtime in the game's own configuration. Exact-owned cleanup restores
  the prior configuration bytes or absence and leaves Steam running.
- The bot supports the distinct ACC customization selector and its popup.
  Its helicopter Back test now follows the actual cancellation dialog, uses
  the effective VR menu stick, and requires the live cabin camera/control owner.

## Validation and remaining work

The build runs all 38 CTest groups and packaged installer transaction checks,
including 138 focused bot checks. Native retained tests include cold completed-
Rival recovery, all four Development category entries, an eligible AM D114 LA
purchase with the exact reviewed GMP/material costs, default/remapped Map
prompts, and helicopter selector entry/discard/cabin/immediate reopening.
The weapon development timer and helicopter upgrade transactions are not
certified by those results.

Matched native field tests compare no extra lens, shared exposure and isolated
exposure. Each exposure run contains 16 scoped samples with both-eye review.
The cold default-on run keeps the head close to its no-lens reference while
the lens still adapts independently. Static captures do not certify continuous
flicker-free presentation, every lighting scene or physical headset readability.

Still open: retail iDroid beam alignment for custom fits and forced tutorial
stow/reopen; cover camera transitions; turret reticles and vertical travel;
binocular housing lighting and intermittent hand/device space reports; FAKEL
support-hand feel; community stair/catwalk stickiness; remaining menu, upgrade,
weapon and headset permutations. See [current status](CURRENT.md).

## Install or update

Close MGSV, extract the complete ZIP and open **MGS5VR-Launcher.exe**. Select
your owned `mgsvtpp.exe`, then choose **Install VR** or **Update / Keep My Settings**.
Connect PC VR, leave Steam signed in and choose **Launch**. `Launch-Headset.cmd`
is also included. Existing personal settings and bindings are preserved.

`RELEASE.json`, `BUILD.json`, `manifest.json` and `SOURCE_REVISION.txt` identify
the exact package and source. The package includes the mod, launcher, source,
licenses and documentation. Required retail assets are imported from the
player's own installation; saves, extracted game assets and private simulator
binaries are not distributed.
