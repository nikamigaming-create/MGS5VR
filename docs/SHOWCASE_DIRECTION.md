# Final combined showcase

User direction, updated 12 September 2026: exercise the weapons and gadgets in
both games, then complete the combined showcase. Ground Zeroes' tracked
first-person camera and rig come last. Its current native third-person stereo
may supply supporting footage, explicitly labeled. Do not present an early
compilation as the final all-equipment showcase or call GZ first-person-ready.

Use the visual language of our instructions: warm off-white, charcoal, restrained
red rules, strong condensed headings, generous readable caption spacing. Use
Snake's likeness in chapter/title overlays, with enough contrast that text never
competes with his face or the gameplay. Native gameplay and audio stay primary.

Alternate matched-action TPP/GZ side-by-side sequences with deliberate wipes into
full-screen TPP or full-screen GZ. Show both games actually doing the actions;
labels identify the game and real current controls. Similar actions across the
two games are editorial pairings, not pixel-parity comparisons.

Capture at the final build's native resolution and frame rate. Preserve aspect
ratio; do not stretch square eye captures to widescreen or call a low-frame-rate
source 60-fps gameplay. Keep action, aiming, wrists and relevant UI inside each
panel's safe area. Use original audio with clean transitions, not two competing
full-volume mixes. Include an indexed full-feature walkthrough as well as the
shorter cinematic edit if needed to make every supported action readable.

Coverage: entry/menus and transitions; head/hands/arms; movement and stances;
each weapon and reload family; scopes/binoculars/marking/intel; equipment and
items; pickup/carry/Fulton/CQC; applicable prosthetic abilities; applicable
buddies/animals; riding, vehicles and mounted weapons. Features exclusive to one
game get that game's full-screen chapter, not a fabricated counterpart.

Existing September 9/12 TPP footage is a source archive, not footage of the final
build. Do not recycle its removed hand-fade or full-screen binocular-zoom behavior
as a current feature. Fresh GZ native stereo rifle, pistol, grenade, flare,
magazine and NVG recordings now exist, with process-only game audio and separate
final-eye stills. Fresh TPP rifle/pistol/switch recordings also exist. These are
partial action observations, not complete per-item passes. Individual take
metadata identifies the exact build and source-eye/final-compositor distinction.
All-arsenal capture and final assembly remain incomplete.
