# Experimental release - 10 October 2026

This prerelease updates VR controls and native weapon, iDroid and vehicle
integration for **The Phantom Pain 1.0.15.4 on Windows x64 with PC OpenXR**.

- The handheld iDroid belongs in the **left bionic hand**. Holding LEFT grip
  gives its menus exclusive navigation and zoom control; releasing it restores
  gameplay ownership. Closing now waits for native input release, addressing
  unwanted reloads. Tab cycling and wrap corrections are included.
- RIGHT grip away from the steering wheel requests the personal weapon through
  the game's native draw/fire path. Its trigger is separated from the pedals;
  LEFT stick provides acceleration/braking after centering. LEFT-hand and
  empty-RIGHT-hand wheel contacts support one- and two-hand steering, with a
  stronger 35-degree range. Vehicle entry and exit use a short Y tap.
- Firearm obstruction checks use the tracked barrel and authored muzzle while
  retaining native physics and projectile collision. The launcher exposes the
  tracked-obstruction option.
- Helicopter deployment retains physical binocular controls and the native Y
  exit. Returning to the ACC shows an iDroid/deployment hint on the left forearm.
- The launcher honors the saved headset runtime, including Meta OpenXR for
  wired Quest Link. Launcher mappings and four PNG control guides reflect the
  effective bindings. Ordinary HUD/status stays on the left forearm; physical
  sights and binoculars remain the intended aiming interfaces.

**Validation:** tested development DLL `0a0d5f0f26cf` passed all **54 automated
test groups** and installer checks. Three actual stationary jeep sidearm shots
consumed 10 → 9 → 8 → 7 rounds. The final four-case run passed sidearm firing
with neutral pedals, LEFT +35-degree steering, both-hand -35-degree steering
and normal Y exit. Earlier DLL `439294973d88` passed scoped iDroid
stow/immediate reopen without ammunition changes, helicopter binocular checks
and clear-muzzle prone firing. Those earlier results remain evidence of that DLL.

**Remaining coverage:** moving-vehicle handling/firing, an actually obstructed
barrel followed by clear firing without changing stance, tab-wrap acceptance,
continuous held-gun visibility, other weapons/tutorials/missions and physical
headset acceptance are incomplete. This is not full-game certification.
Historical hand/arm rig, iDroid mount/stow/tutorial animation and frame-pacing
regressions remain in the readiness catalog without current continuous acceptance.
The final `BUILD.json` and `RELEASE.json` manifests identify the packaged source
and binaries.

**Install/update:** close MGSV, extract the entire archive and open
**MGS5VR-Launcher.exe**. Select your owned `mgsvtpp.exe`, then choose **Install VR**
or **Update / Keep My Settings**. Meta Quest Link users should select
**Meta OpenXR**. Keep Steam running and signed in.
