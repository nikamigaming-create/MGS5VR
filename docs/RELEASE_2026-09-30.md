# Experimental release - 30 September 2026

This update restores the left-arm readout, repairs the handheld iDroid's native
attachment, brings physical binoculars closer to the face and adds optional
weapon stabilization and a launcher controls tour. It also includes the retained
Coco contributions, physical binocular lesson integration and local native A*
navigation tooling developed since the September 24 public build.

Supported game: The Phantom Pain 1.0.15.4, Windows x64, PC OpenXR headset.
This is an experimental prerelease. Whole-game completion, every weapon and
physical headset acceptance remain open.

## Changes

- The weapon/status readout and ordinary HUD remain on the **left bionic arm**.
  The compact readout again uses its authored native layout.
- Handheld iDroid uses the measured native right-wrist attachment and the
  device's named connector and hologram sockets. Normal controller grip holds
  it upright; the screen inherits the rendered device pose. Pause remains a
  spatial stereo panel. Handheld iDroid is optional (`settings.handheld_menus=1`);
  existing settings are preserved during updates.
- The simulator high-resolution swapchain repair preserves native scene detail
  and filters each eye separately. Desktop preview remains **1280 x 720**;
  physical VR retains runtime eye FOVs and its separate native rendering path.
- Physical binoculars retain hand aiming, zoom, enemy acquisition and explicit
  marking. The rear safety clearance is 3 cm, independently of the optical
  reference, so the ocular can be brought closer to the face.
- Optional adaptive weapon smoothing keeps the weapon, sight and aim in one
  solved frame. The public default is off; tune Weapon smoothing in the launcher.
- Ten capacitive contacts are independently mappable: A/B/X/Y, both sticks,
  both triggers and both thumb rests. Touch and press remain separate inputs.
- The launcher controls tour shows saved bindings, alternatives, one-hand and
  two-hand chords, amber contact surfaces and red pressed surfaces. Bindings
  remain editable and validated; updates preserve personal controls.
- The bounded test runner releases inputs, restores poses and closes test-opened
  menus. Local development updates the same `play/` folder in place, preserving
  an unchanged open launcher. Steam does not need to be restarted.
- Clean source builds show unavailable historical evidence explicitly in the
  launcher. Present evidence still has to match its recorded file hash.

## Validation and known issues

All **38 local CTest groups** and packaged installer transaction checks pass,
including 93 focused bot checks. Native Continue reached Mission 6 and all 11
selected iDroid/menu cases passed on the gameplay DLL below. Both compositor
eyes were reviewed for centred normal grip, opposing side views, ordinary
close/immediate reopening and pause. Sequential stills establish scoped fit;
they do not certify continuous refresh or physical headset readability.

Historical display captures contain no observed blank frame across 308 decoded
samples. Mission 1 binocular lessons and the Mission 6 bridge event were tested
on earlier identified candidates. Those results do not certify entire missions
or prove every interaction on the new release.

Still open:

- Cover transitions can pull the camera unexpectedly; native cover is retained.
- Mounted turret reticles and vertical travel need native-state reproduction.
- Binocular housing/lens lighting does not yet fully match the native world;
  intermittent hand/device space reports need transition testing.
- FAKEL (SLEEP) support-hand grip needs reproduction and repair.
- Forced FOB tutorial iDroid stow/reopen, off-centre/close screen fit, per-weapon
  sight/muzzle/hit alignment and full-game/headset acceptance need further work.

## Install or update

Close MGSV, extract the complete ZIP and open **MGS5VR-Launcher.exe**. Select
your owned `mgsvtpp.exe`, then choose **Install VR** for a new install or
**Update / Keep My Settings** for an existing install. Connect PC VR, leave
Steam signed in and choose **Launch**. `Launch-Headset.cmd` is also included.

The package contains compiled mod/launcher tools, complete committed source,
licenses and documentation. Binocular assets are imported locally from your
own game. Retail archives, extracted game models/textures, saves, private
navigation and simulator/operator binaries are not distributed.
