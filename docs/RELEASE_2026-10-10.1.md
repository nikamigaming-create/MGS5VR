# Experimental hotfix - 10 October 2026, patch 1

Fixes the spatial iDroid Development freeze and ordinary ACC stow/reopen for
The Phantom Pain 1.0.15.4 on Windows x64 with PC OpenXR.

- Spatial iDroid now retains the game's native world-update policy. The mod's
  extra tutorial pause was stopping Development child pages from processing
  their model and exit work, even while the game and UI kept rendering.
- Ordinary ACC closure requests native stow before finishing the terminal.
  The first Menu tap can reopen the iDroid after closing it.

The freeze was reproduced in the actual game on Mother Base: short Back could
not leave Helicopter Development while the mod-owned pause was active. Removing
only that registration immediately restored Back; restoring it reproduced the
failure. With the fix, spatial Weapons/Items, Buddy and Helicopter scrolling and
Back were checked in the ACC and on Mother Base. Weapon dialogs, ACC suit/gear
navigation, and native stow followed by first-tap reopening were also exercised.
The original save was restored after testing.

Automated checks and installer validation pass. These are scoped simulator
checks on late-game saves. An archived save was also tested, but it had the same
late-game progression; early-game Development lessons, every upgrade transaction
and physical-headset acceptance remain unverified. Existing experimental limits
include free-roam tab wrap, long-gun visibility in vehicles and moving-vehicle
firing. This hotfix does not claim full-game certification.

Close MGSV, extract the whole archive, open **MGS5VR-Launcher.exe**, and choose
**Update / Keep My Settings**. For wired Quest Link select **Meta OpenXR** and
keep Steam running. The packaged BUILD.json and RELEASE.json identify the exact
source and binaries.
