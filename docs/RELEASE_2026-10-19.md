# Experimental update - 19 October 2026

Updates The Phantom Pain 1.0.15.4 on Windows x64 with PC OpenXR.

## Changes

- Rocket/Blast Arm guidance is presented through the left-hand iDroid projection while Snake's body and the surrounding world remain live.
- Full-body and cabin rig improvements, including retention of the existing native hip weapon mount when the full body is admitted.
- Cabin prop interaction, iDroid handling, native menu routing and controls-editor improvements.
- Physical weapon, binocular and optical presentation fixes, with ordinary HUD retained on the left bionic forearm and Pause on a spatial panel.

## Validation and known issues

The user reports completing headset testing and authorized this release. This is not a claim that every mission, weapon or interaction passed headset testing. Automated build and installer checks are required for the packaged candidate; their exact results are recorded in BUILD.json.

Scoped simulator evidence covers Rocket Arm launch, guidance, ground impact and return with both-eye iDroid presentation; cabin body poses, prop interactions, menu round trips and controls editing have separate scoped evidence. Destroying a target with the Rocket Arm was not proven.

**Ground-turret and helicopter-door-gun hand snapping and hand aiming are not implemented.** Existing stick controls remain; this release does not certify either mounted weapon class. The tested mounted-grip helper is not connected to live turret aiming.

Full ACC loadout visibility and physical holster grabbing are unfinished. Existing field weapon mounts do not establish complete wearable equipment in the cabin. Some forced tutorials, menu branches and full-mission transitions remain unverified. The package retains documented blocked and pending regression coverage rather than claiming full-game acceptance. See CURRENT.md for evidence and remaining work.

## Installation

Close MGSV and extract the entire archive. Open MGS5VR-Launcher.exe, select your legally owned game and choose Update / Keep My Settings. Wired Quest Link users should select Meta OpenXR. Personal settings, saves and retail assets are not included.

BUILD.json, RELEASE.json, SOURCE_REVISION.txt, manifest.json and the embedded source archive identify the checked payload. This is an experimental prerelease.
