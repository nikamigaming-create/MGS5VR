## Launch and controls

On the development PC, this DLL is already installed. Connect PC VR and open
`Launch-Headset.cmd` in the v11 package. Its configuration check resolved the
Oculus physical OpenXR runtime. The SIM/game session is closed for handoff;
Steam remains running. No global runtime setting was changed.

On another installation, extract the complete package, open the launcher,
select TPP, and choose **Update / Keep My Settings**. The active controls file
is `mgs5vr-controls.ini` beside the game's DLL. Snap turning is opt-in; the
current installed setting is `native_smooth`. This playtest package enables
the interactive cabin by default; updates preserve existing preferences.

1. Enter the cabin, move with the left stick, reach Continue until highlighted,
   then squeeze that hand's trigger. Press A when loading offers Resume.
2. Raise and rotate the left arm. Hold LT alone for the four native equipment
   cards. Right-stick directions enter categories; B backs out; release LT to
   close. Check that the permanent HUD stays flat on the forearm.
3. Tap left Menu for iDroid. Cup the right palm, pan with the right stick, zoom
   with triggers, and press B to close. Check attachment and readability.
4. Hold left grip + Y for binoculars; left-stick click cycles 2×/4×. Check both
   eyes, then mark with RT and clear with A. B stows the device.
5. Ready a weapon with right grip, fire with RT, and reload with left grip +
   a short B tap. Test pistol and scoped rifle alignment against a visible
   target. Move hands near the HMD, above the head, and below the waist.
6. Hold left Menu for Pause; B resumes. Check one guard interrogation and the
   resulting iDroid intel. Report the first blocker and the action that caused it.

## What passed on this DLL

- All 16 CTest suites and the assembled package's install/update/rollback tests.
- Cold start, cabin entry, tracked Continue highlight/trigger, native loading
  confirmation and mission 30010 FreePlay.
- LT neutral preview, all four actual native categories, browsing, B back,
  release/reopen, independent head/arm movement, and forearm HUD attachment.
- Ordinary iDroid pan/zoom, handheld movement and B close. Zoomed geometry
  remains within the projected panel bounds in the observed views.
- Both-eye binocular imagery, 2×/4× change, lowering/stowing and visible world.
- Pistol fire and reload with native ammo changes, plus ordinary locomotion.
  Initial neutral SIM grip pointed the gun downward; the corrected controller
  pose presents it forward. Physical controller fit and hit accuracy need testing.
- World remained visible at the sampled near-head/above-head/below-waist hand
  poses and head turn. This does not test real tracking occlusion or HMD removal.

## Known failures and remaining coverage

- **Pause:** native arms freeze. The menu retains that arm's anchor, but it can
  occupy too much of the view and extend beyond the visible edge. Full paused
  arm tracking and menu fit are unfinished. B resume worked.
- **FOB tutorial:** this save forced the retail tutorial, which blocked normal
  tab/back navigation. A diagnostic native terminal-close call was needed to
  test the ordinary map; no tutorial-completion flags were changed. This flow
  has not passed using controllers alone.
- **Clipping:** shaders unavailable to reflection use a projected rectangular
  bound. Exact rotated edge clipping is tested on the GPU, but is not established
  for those legacy game shaders; diagonal corner overdraw can remain.
- **Notifications:** full caption/notification routing is incomplete. The
  recorded Intel Team caption appeared away from the forearm; do not claim all
  personal HUD is already wrist-bound.
- Guard mark/unmark/intel reveal, scoped-rifle hit accuracy, runtime tracking
  loss, all story transitions, mounted guns/vehicles and every equipment variant
  are not current-candidate passes. Quiet/two pickup rats and universal mod
  installation remain unfinished. GZ is not a complete tracked first-person port.

No replacement save, forced unlock or tutorial completion was installed. Native
gameplay can autosave; a checkpoint occurred during the locomotion check.
