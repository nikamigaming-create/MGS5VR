# Experimental update - 11 October 2026

This candidate updates The Phantom Pain 1.0.15.4 on Windows x64 with PC OpenXR.
It retains physical weapons and binoculars, the left bionic forearm HUD, the
left-hand handheld iDroid and spatial Pause presentation.

## Changes

- D-Dog remains visible in the native ACC before opening and after closing
  iDroid. The native camera-nearness fade now uses the rendered VR cabin camera
  position. Native actor animation, fade distances and publication remain intact.
- Opening handheld iDroid preserves the headset's orientation reference.
  Fresh movement after stow releases the mod-owned handset input block.
- With handheld iDroid open, LEFT stick moves Snake until LEFT grip is held.
  While focused, LEFT stick browses lists and RIGHT stick left/right selects
  Previous/Next tab. Center the stick before repeating. The RIGHT-grip page loop
  and LEFT-grip + LEFT-stick-click Previous alternative remain available.
- Clearing a binocular enemy mark with A prevents automatic reacquisition on
  look-away/return. RIGHT trigger can explicitly mark that enemy again. Optional
  world enemy labels are smaller and follow native acquired enemy positions.
- Native NVG goggles no longer obstruct the VR camera. The native night-vision
  effect and equip/on/off behavior remain active.
- Rewards confirmation recognizes the native bonus-popup owner and accepts the
  existing VR Confirm binding on the spatial stereo panel.
- Helicopter passenger movement follows the carrier before headset rotation,
  preserving the seated offset during head turns, pitch and leaning.
- The launcher exposes God mode, Enemy markers in the world and Block firing
  near cover. God mode and world labels default Off; cover firing blocking
  defaults Off. Bullet collision and native ammunition rules remain intact.
- The launcher guide shows the current tab chords and keeps thumb rests,
  stick-top contact, trigger contact and face-button contact available to bind.
  The bounded startup runner handles successive native Online Information pages.

## Validation and limits

The checked candidate passed all 55 automated groups and packaged installer
checks. The real launcher and native settings bridge passed 217 checks across
104 actions, 59 settings, 29 physical inputs, ten touch contacts and ten axes.

Actual-game simulator checks reviewed both compositor eyes for five D-Dog
closed/open/stowed/turn/lean checkpoints and 14 field marker/iDroid outcomes.
LEFT movement with the handset open covered 2.42 m; focused RIGHT-stick tabs,
LEFT list navigation, the retained grip loop and stow/next-tap reopening passed.
Physical binocular acquisition, A clear, look-away/return without reacquisition,
explicit re-marking and compact world labels passed. Earlier scoped checks
covered NVG, Rewards, passenger movement, native enemy damage with God mode
On/Off and close-cover firing On/Off.

These are scoped checks. The intermittent reported checkpoint loading hang has
not been reproduced on a matching save. An isolated native failure-panel/retry
case remains unresolved. Physical Meta Quest Link acceptance, complete missions,
every weapon/vehicle/forced tutorial, pickup/return helicopter comfort, rain NVG
presentation and very rapid close-during-reopen timing remain unverified.
Music stop/reopen checks did not reproduce the reported lockup; its specific
source-video cause is not proven. The package manifest preserves documented
blocked and pending regression coverage; this update does not claim full-game
or physical-headset certification.

## Install or update

Close MGSV and extract the whole archive. Open **MGS5VR-Launcher.exe**, select
your legally owned game and choose **Update / Keep My Settings**. For wired
Quest Link select **Meta OpenXR**, then launch with Steam running. Retain the
whole package, including its tools and controller assets.

Controller bindings and VR settings are editable in the launcher. Save, release
all buttons/grips/touch contacts and center the sticks for about two seconds to
apply a changed layout. Public defaults are included; personal settings and
saves are not shipped. The existing user's handheld preference is retained by
the settings-preserving update path.

`BUILD.json`, `RELEASE.json`, `SOURCE_REVISION.txt`, the embedded source archive
and `manifest.json` identify the exact checked source and payload. The narrated
controller-mapping walkthrough is supplied separately as
`MGS5VR-controller-mapping.mp4`; its earlier actual-game segment is explicitly
labeled as the retained grip/click alternatives. See
[the controller guide](CONTROLS.md), [mapping review](CONTROLLER_MAPPING_REGRESSION.md),
[field fixes](FIELD_IDROID_COMBAT_FIXES.md), [D-Dog correction](CABIN_DOG_VISIBILITY_FIX.md)
and [NVG correction](NVG_VISIBILITY_FIX.md).

Preparing this archive does not publish it or replace the last published local
`play/` pin. Both local Play paths continue to use Meta Quest Link.
