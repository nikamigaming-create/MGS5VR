# Experimental comfort update - 19 October 2026

Adds seated play and targeted comfort fixes for The Phantom Pain 1.0.15.4 on Windows x64 with PC OpenXR.

## Changes

- **Seated mode:** enable Seated mode on the first VR Settings page and Apply while sitting. Your seated position becomes the baseline for native standing height. Recenter recalibrates without asking you to stand; physical lean and native crouch/prone remain available.
- **Recenter height:** preserves the native anatomical height and posture reference instead of recalibrating from a transient lowered animation frame.
- **Idle controllers:** accepts runtime-valid inferred poses when optical tracking flags drop, targeting the reported idle-controller view fallback. Invalid poses still release controls.
- **Crawling:** stabilizes the camera against alternating chest yaw using the actor's root frame.
- **Support hand:** retains contact in the weapon attachment frame as that attachment moves relative to the wrist. Native reload and bolt manipulation remain available.

## Validation and limitations

The user reports testing this candidate and authorized publication. All 58 automated groups and installer checks passed. This is not a claim that every reported symptom, headset, mission or weapon has been independently verified.

Existing limitations remain: physical hand aiming for ground turrets and helicopter guns, complete ACC holster grabbing and loadout visibility are unfinished. Broader tutorial and mission coverage remains open. Existing physical binocular controls, left-hand iDroid and left-forearm HUD are preserved.

## Installation

Close MGSV, extract the complete archive and open MGS5VR-Launcher.exe. Select your legally owned game and choose Update / Keep My Settings. Wired Quest Link users should use Meta OpenXR. Seated mode defaults off; enable it while sitting.

BUILD.json, RELEASE.json, SOURCE_REVISION.txt, manifest.json and the embedded source archive identify the checked payload. This remains an experimental prerelease.
