# Experimental patch 2026-10-02.1

This patch updates tracked hand poses, native iDroid attachment and menu
behavior. It also changes the GPU frame handoff and improves recording and
regression diagnostics. It remains an experimental prerelease for The Phantom
Pain 1.0.15.4 on Windows x64 with PC OpenXR.

## Implemented changes

- Grip and aim retention use paired poses for both hands, with ownership and
  freshness checks during tracking interruptions.
- The right-hand handheld iDroid is the public default. Explicit spatial-mode
  preferences are preserved. Device UI uses the source-matched native handset
  pose, and native body/equipment publication remains responsible for its
  authored alignment and animation.
- The handheld opening boundary defers a separate body refresh when the
  native update is within 150 ms, preserving its equipment publication.
  Five additional guarded chrome layouts bring device routing to 25 verified
  layouts. Ordinary HUD and status remain on the left forearm; shared Mission
  Orders content is excluded from device-only suppression.
- Spatial iDroid no longer registers the mod-owned world pause during verified
  native cabin play. Field spatial pausing remains, and closing releases that
  registration before the native open flag clears. This targets the reproduced
  ACC Development Back failure, with scoped child-page results below.
- Stereo transfer uses a bounded three-slot handoff with per-owner GPU
  completion fences and a guarded event-query fallback. Exact frame identity,
  eye pairing, freshness, recommended eye resolution and eye FOV are retained.
- Recording preserves source order, reports scheduled losses and explicit
  repeated frames, and writes complete metadata atomically. Performance
  reports separate layer preparation from the exact OpenXR submission call.

Physical firearm sights and handheld binocular aiming, zoom, acquisition and
marking remain the intended interaction paths. Floating firearm reticles stay
off. The desktop preview remains 1280 x 720; VR uses the runtime's recommended
eye images and eye FOVs.

## Validation and known issues

Installed candidate `92d1a69368bd` passed 44 CTest groups and installer
transactions, including 120 real D3D11 mailbox checks. `BUILD.json` and
`RELEASE.json` record the final package's committed source and build identity.

Reviewed ACC runs returned from Weapons/Items to the Development categories
after **one ordinary 120 ms VR Back in both spatial and handheld modes**.
Spatial Buddy Equipment also returned with one Back; both eyes were reviewed
for these outcomes. The spatial run passed 13 state cases and the handheld
run passed eight. A subsequent handheld category Down check reported that the
menu stick stayed held after release; input was neutralized and the owned test
session closed safely. Its cause remains unknown. Handheld Buddy Equipment
and both-mode Helicopter/Security exits remain to be checked.

Seven handheld field cases also passed: open, cant, right/left yaw, ordinary
Back, immediate reopening and final stow. All 14 outcome images were reviewed;
the open phone is full-sized, both arms remain visible, and stow clears its UI.
Initial/reopen left-eye images caught the UI fade before the later right-eye
images settled.

Four spatial field cases passed as well: open, one 120 ms Back, immediate
reopen and final 120 ms Back. All eight outcome images retain both arms and
show the Map open or its UI cleared as expected. The final handset is mid-stow
in the left capture and gone in the later right capture. These sequential
outcomes do not verify simultaneous stereo, continuous transitions or paused
world motion.

A bounded simulator check passed 12 tracking state cases, including five
independent pose-loss/recovery periods of at least 5.5 seconds after opening
iDroid. All ten loss and ten recovery images retain both arms, the full-size
phone, Map and world. Loss observations stay inside their leases and the raw
tracking flags recover afterward.
Physical Quest behavior, opening with lost tracking and continuous animation
remain untested by this check.

Prior candidate `7a5b00774fd2` passed seven ordinary field state cases with
both outcome eyes reviewed. Its recording still showed a small detached device
at first opening and Map chrome on the left forearm for one closing source.
The current boundary/layout fixes target these defects; the new outcome
images do not resolve those exact recorded boundaries. Earlier sustained
motion and intermittent arm visibility failures remain in the coverage ledger.

OpenXR submission stalls remain unresolved; a prior capture-off field window
reached 811.755 ms in `xrEndFrame`. Current-candidate performance and physical
Quest motion, readability and performance need testing. Supply-drop cue
visibility, intermittent weapon selection, forced tutorial transitions and
cutscene camera/animation reports remain open. Whole-game, all-weapon and
physical-headset coverage is incomplete.

Known issues and incomplete coverage remain recorded in `RELEASE.json` and the
public regression catalog. See [current status](CURRENT.md) and
[regression coverage](REGRESSION_COVERAGE.md) for their scope.

## Install or update

Close MGSV, extract the complete archive and open **MGS5VR-Launcher.exe**.
Choose your owned `mgsvtpp.exe`, then **Install VR** or **Update / Keep My
Settings**. Existing personal settings and bindings are preserved. Connect
PC VR, leave Steam signed in and launch normally.

`RELEASE.json`, `BUILD.json`, `manifest.json`, `SOURCE_REVISION.txt` and the
included source archive identify the package. Retail assets and saves are
not distributed.
